Pool House Design & Construction, Start to Finish

A pool house lives between indoors and out. We design it to match the main house, then build the plumbing, gas and electrical it needs for a bath, a kitchen or a bar.

Wide openings, covered space and durable finishes make it a room you use all year, not only in summer.

  • Design to match the main house
  • Bathrooms and changing rooms
  • Outdoor kitchen and bar
  • Covered patios and doors that open fully
  • Electrical, plumbing and gas
  • Permits and inspections
