Restoration & Fire Rebuilds, Start to Finish

After a fire or major damage, the work starts before the build: assessing what can be saved, documenting the loss, and working with your insurance adjuster on scope. We take on that coordination so you are not managing it alone.

For older homes, restoration means repairing structure and systems while keeping the trim, windows and floors that make the house what it is.

  • Fire and damage assessment
  • Working directly with your insurance adjuster
  • Structural, foundation and seismic repair
  • Rewiring and repiping
  • Matching original trim, windows and flooring
  • Permits and inspections through the rebuild
