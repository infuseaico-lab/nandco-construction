How It Runs

How a Project Runs

Three stages, with a written estimate and schedule up front.

  1. 1

    Site Visit and Estimate

    We walk the property with you, measure, look at the structure and systems, and talk through what you want. A line-item estimate follows, with no hidden markups and no vague allowances.

  2. 2

    Design and Permits

    You approve the plans and every finish. We file with LADBS or your city's building department and answer every plan-check comment, while our purchase coordinators order your selections.

  3. 3

    Build and Walkthrough

    Your project manager runs the site with our vetted trades, every inspection is passed, and we close the punch list with you before handover.

FAQ

The questions homeowners ask us first.

  • (213) 444-6596
  • CA license #1044615
  • Los Angeles County
Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We prepare the plans, file with LADBS or your own city's building department (Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, Pasadena, Burbank, Malibu and others run their own), and handle every inspection through final sign-off.

Do you work with insurance after a fire?

Yes. After a fire or major damage we work directly with your insurance adjuster on the scope of the rebuild, so you are not managing the claim and the construction alone.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the work. Including design and permits, a bathroom typically takes two to three months, a kitchen three to five and an ADU eight to twelve. You get a written schedule after the site visit.

How do you price a project?

With a line-item estimate after the site visit: no hidden markups and no vague allowances, so you can see where every part of the budget goes.

Who do I talk to during the project?

Your project coordinator is your point of contact from start to finish. A project manager runs the site, and the owner, Tony Nguyen, answers for the whole job.

Can I build an ADU on my lot?

Often, yes. We start with a feasibility review of your lot and what your city allows, then design the detached ADU, garage conversion or junior ADU that fits.

How do I check your license?

Search license #1044615 (Nandco LLC) on the Contractors State License Board website.
