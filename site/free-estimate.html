What we check for your free estimate

Every home is different, so on the visit we check six things before we write anything down.

  • Size and scope

    The rooms or the part of the lot involved, and whether the floor plan stays put or changes.

  • Structure and systems

    Walls, foundation, plumbing, gas and electrical: moving or replacing them shapes the job more than any finish.

  • Permits

    What LADBS or your city's building department asks for at your address, and whether the plans go through plan check.

  • Finish level

    Cabinets, stone, tile and fixtures: the choices that change the budget the most.

  • The house itself

    Older Los Angeles homes can hide outdated wiring, galvanized pipe and unpermitted work. We check before we price.

  • Insurance claims

    After a fire or damage, we review the scope with your adjuster so the rebuild is covered properly.

From first visit to handover

  1. The first visit

    We walk the property with you, measure, and look at the structure and systems.

  2. Drawings

    Measured plans and elevations, so you see the project before it is built.

  3. Selections

    Cabinets, stone, tile, fixtures and lighting, chosen side by side and ordered by our purchase coordinators.

  4. Permits

    We file with LADBS or your city and answer every plan-check comment.

  5. Structure and rough-in

    Framing, beams, new circuits, water and gas run where the plans put them.

  6. Finishes

    Cabinets, counters, tile, floors and paint, installed by our vetted trades.

  7. Final and handover

    Final inspection signed, punch list closed with you, and the house handed over clean.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the work. Including design and permits, a bathroom typically takes two to three months, a kitchen three to five and an ADU eight to twelve. You get a written schedule after the site visit.

Do you work with insurance after a fire?

Yes. We work directly with your insurance adjuster on the scope of the rebuild, so you are not managing the claim and the construction alone.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We prepare the plans, file with LADBS or your own city's building department, and handle every inspection through final sign-off.

Can I build an ADU on my lot?

Often, yes. We start with a feasibility review of your lot and what your city allows, then design the detached ADU, garage conversion or junior ADU that fits.

How do you price a project?

With a line-item estimate after the site visit: no hidden markups and no vague allowances, so you can see where every part of the budget goes.

What happens after I request an estimate?

We call to book a site visit. After the walkthrough you get a written, line-item estimate that is yours to keep, with no obligation.
